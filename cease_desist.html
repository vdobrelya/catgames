Notice to Cease and Desist

❧

To the Dragon presently in occupation, without lease, licence, or leave, of a cavern at the summit of the Mountain, hereinafter “the Respondent”:

Take notice that on a night now one week past, of which you will need no reminding, you did wilfully, wantonly, and with every appearance of enjoyment, set ablaze the greater part of the village of Dorfenburg, to wit: the dwellings, the Millhouse, the tanner’s yard, the offices of the Dorfenburg Crier, and sundry outbuildings, fences, hayricks of this village, and one (1) much-lov’d elderly goat, answering to Brunhilde. Such conduct constitutes arson, trespass, destruction of property, and manners of a kind this office had not thought possible in a creature of your evident age.

The Respondent is hereby directed to immediately cease all incineration, scorching, smouldering, and ominous circling of this village and its environs, and any other conduct of a like nature, whether presently contemplated or not.

Failure to comply will result in the full weight of Dorfenburg’s remedies, including but not limited to military action, reports to the Crown, and other means mystical, magical, religious, and agricultural.

Restitution for the goat is non-negotiable.

Govern yourself accordingly.

By Order of the Mayor

Office of the Mayor, Dorfenburg

(per the Mayor’s secretary, who asks that this not be mention’d to the brewer)
